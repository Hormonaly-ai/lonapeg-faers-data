# LONAPEG-FAERS protocol v1.0 (prospective; registered before any lonapegsomatropin-specific FAERS query or analysis)

**Title:** Post-marketing safety of lonapegsomatropin (Skytrofa, lonapegsomatropin-tcgd): a disproportionality analysis of the FDA Adverse Event Reporting System (FAERS) with active comparators and a systematic review of published case reports and clinical-trial safety data

**Protocol date:** 23 November 2026. **Status at registration:** No lonapegsomatropin-specific FAERS query, extraction, count or analysis has been run or viewed for this study, and no literature screening has been done. Disclosure of prior data contact: (i) the same team downloaded the FDA FAERS/legacy AERS quarterly ASCII files 2010 Q4-2026 Q3 earlier on 23 November 2026 for a registered tesamorelin study (OSF 10.17605/OSF.IO/H5PBN) and reduced them to parquet tables; these processed tables will be reused here rather than downloaded again. In that study the somatropin comparator was defined by a text pattern containing "SOMATROPIN", which also matches "LONAPEGSOMATROPIN"; any lonapegsomatropin reports were therefore pooled, unidentified, inside that study's somatropin comparator, and no lonapegsomatropin-specific count, PT or report was extracted or viewed. (ii) During the novelty search (23 November 2026) two public aggregator web pages displayed openFDA all-years, non-deduplicated totals for lonapegsomatropin in search-result excerpts (612 reports, 8% serious, 0 deaths, 16% female on one page; 854 reports, 80 serious, 0 deaths on another); no PT-level counts were viewed. (iii) The current Skytrofa US label (BLA 761177, openFDA version 14, effective 21 October 2026) was read to prespecify labeled terms. No dedicated lonapegsomatropin FAERS, VigiBase or EudraVigilance disproportionality study was found (PubMed, Europe PMC including preprints, web and congress-abstract search, 23 November 2026). Code (FAERS reduction, deduplication, disproportionality statistics, MGPS fitting) is reused from the tesamorelin study.

**Reporting guidelines:** READUS-PV (2024) for the disproportionality analysis; PRISMA 2020 for the review; JBI critical-appraisal checklists for case reports and case series; Naranjo scale for causality; McHarm items for harms reporting in trials.

## 1. Background and rationale
Lonapegsomatropin is a long-acting prodrug of somatropin: unmodified somatropin is transiently bound to a methoxypolyethylene glycol (mPEG) carrier through a TransCon linker and released over about a week. The FDA approved it (Skytrofa, BLA 761177) in August 2021 for pediatric growth hormone deficiency (GHD) and extended the indication to adult GHD in 2025 (label revision 7/2025); the European Commission authorised it in January 2022. It is given once weekly with a dedicated auto-injector and dual-chamber cartridges. Its US label carries the somatropin class warnings (neoplasms, glucose intolerance/diabetes, intracranial hypertension, fluid retention, hypoadrenalism, hypothyroidism, slipped capital femoral epiphysis, scoliosis progression, pancreatitis, lipoatrophy, hypersensitivity). The EU risk-management plan lists neoplasms, type 2 diabetes mellitus, medication errors and long-term safety including reactions potentially related to mPEG exposure as potential risks; EU periodic safety reviews added hypersensitivity and osteonecrosis. Weekly dosing produces supraphysiological IGF-1 peaks and a new device and dosing schedule, both plausible sources of new reporting patterns. FAERS studies of growth hormone as a class and of somatrogon exist, but none has analysed lonapegsomatropin as its own product or against the other long-acting products.

## 2. Objectives
Primary: to identify disproportionate reporting of adverse events (MedDRA Preferred Terms, PTs) for lonapegsomatropin in FAERS from approval (FDA receipt 2021 Q3) to the latest available quarter (2026 Q3), versus all other drugs reported in the same period.
Secondary: (a) compare lonapegsomatropin with active comparators reported in the same period: daily somatropin (GHD products), somatrogon and, descriptively, somapacitan; (b) describe report characteristics (sex, age, reporter type, country, quarter, seriousness and outcomes, indication); (c) evaluate the EU RMP potential risks (neoplasms, type 2 diabetes/hyperglycaemia, medication errors, long-term safety/mPEG) and the label class risks (injection-site reactions and lipoatrophy, hypersensitivity, slipped capital femoral epiphysis/osteonecrosis, scoliosis, intracranial hypertension, IGF-1 increase, hypothyroidism, adrenal insufficiency, pancreatitis); (d) separate dosing, device and product-handling PTs from clinical events and describe them; (e) compare pediatric (<18 years) and adult (>=18 years) reports, including before and after the 2025 adult indication; (f) describe the quarterly reporting pattern and characterise any surge, including 2026 Q3; (g) estimate time to onset; (h) classify signals as labeled or unlabeled against the current US label; (i) systematically review published case reports/series and clinical-trial and registry safety data for lonapegsomatropin, and FDA/EMA/TGA regulatory review documents.

## 3. Hypotheses (prespecified)
H1: Injection-site reactions and IGF-1 increased will show disproportionality (ROR lower 95% CI > 1) versus all drugs.
H2: Dose omission, device and product-handling PTs will account for a large share of lonapegsomatropin reports (descriptive) and will be reported disproportionately versus all drugs.
H3 (non-directional, versus daily somatropin and somatrogon): injection-site reactions, lipoatrophy, hypersensitivity, hyperglycaemia/diabetes, IGF-1 increased, medication/device errors.
H4 (exploratory): neoplasms, slipped capital femoral epiphysis/osteonecrosis, scoliosis, intracranial hypertension, hypothyroidism, adrenal insufficiency and pancreatitis; any finding is hypothesis-generating.
H5 (descriptive): adult reports will rise after the 2025 adult indication; the 2026 Q3 count will be compared with the mean of the previous four quarters and its composition described.

## 4. Data source (Part A)
FDA public quarterly data extracts, ASCII files (https://fis.fda.gov/extensions/FPD-QDE-FAERS/FPD-QDE-FAERS.html), FAERS 2012 Q4-2026 Q3, already downloaded and reduced (2010 Q4-2026 Q3) for the tesamorelin study (download log and file MD5s in that study's records). Analysis window: reports whose retained (latest) version was received by FDA in 2021 Q3-2026 Q3 (21 quarters). Tables: DEMO, DRUG (all reports: primary ID, role, drug key = PROD_AI else DRUGNAME), REAC, OUTC, and full DRUG/THER/INDI detail rows for reports naming any product whose name contains "SOMATROPIN" (this captures lonapegsomatropin when PROD_AI is coded). If an extraction gap is found (e.g., reports coded as "SKYTROFA" in DRUGNAME with PROD_AI present but different), the affected quarters will be re-processed from the FDA files and this will be reported.

## 5. Deduplication and cleaning
1. As in the tesamorelin study: for each CASEID, keep the record with the highest CASEVERSION (ties: latest FDA_DT, then highest PRIMARYID), across all quarters 2010 Q4-2026 Q3; FAERS versions take precedence over legacy AERS; remove CASEIDs listed in any deleted-case file.
2. Restrict to retained reports whose retained version falls in 2021 Q3-2026 Q3. The background for all-drug disproportionality is all retained reports in this window with at least one PT.
3. PTs upper-cased and trimmed; one count per report per PT.
4. Probable-duplicate check within the lonapegsomatropin series: identical sex, age, country, event date and PT set flagged and removed in a sensitivity analysis.

## 6. Case definition and comparators
Lonapegsomatropin report: a retained report with a DRUG row whose drug key (PROD_AI, else DRUGNAME) or DRUGNAME matches `LONAPEGSOMATROPIN|SKYTROFA|TRANSCON\s*(H?GH|GROWTH)|ACP-?011` (case-insensitive).
- Primary analysis: lonapegsomatropin as primary suspect (ROLE_COD = PS). Secondary: any suspect (PS/SS). Sensitivity: any role.
- Daily somatropin comparator (PS): drug key matching `SOMATROPIN|GENOTROPIN|HUMATROPE|NORDITROPIN|NUTROPIN|OMNITROPE|SAIZEN|ZOMACTON|TEV-TROPIN|ACCRETROPIN|VALTROPIN` and not matching the lonapegsomatropin pattern, SOMAPACITAN, SOGROYA, SOMATROGON, NGENLA, SEROSTIM or ZORBTIVE (Serostim and Zorbtive have non-GHD indications; included in a sensitivity analysis).
- Somatrogon comparator (PS): `SOMATROGON|NGENLA`.
- Somapacitan (PS): `SOMAPACITAN|SOGROYA`; reported descriptively (counts and proportions with Wilson 95% CIs) because few reports are expected; a comparator ROR is computed only if >= 100 somapacitan PS reports exist, and then as supplementary.
- Reports naming lonapegsomatropin together with another GH product (switches) are kept in each series and removed in a sensitivity analysis.

## 7. Outcomes and prespecified event groups
Event unit: MedDRA PT as coded (one count per report per PT). No licensed MedDRA hierarchy is assumed; prespecified custom PT groups (exact strings unless stated; clear synonyms may be added and are documented in code):
- Injection-site reactions: any PT beginning "Injection site".
- Lipoatrophy/lipohypertrophy: Lipoatrophy; Injection site atrophy; Lipohypertrophy; Lipodystrophy acquired; Fat tissue decreased; Skin atrophy; Injection site lipoatrophy; Injection site lipohypertrophy.
- Hypersensitivity: Hypersensitivity; Drug hypersensitivity; Anaphylactic reaction; Anaphylactic shock; Anaphylactoid reaction; Urticaria; Rash; Rash pruritic; Rash erythematous; Rash generalised; Rash macular; Rash papular; Pruritus; Pruritus generalised; Erythema; Angioedema; Swelling face; Lip swelling; Flushing.
- Neoplasm (any, incl. benign/unspecified; EU RMP): any PT containing NEOPLASM, TUMOUR, CARCINOMA, CANCER, LYMPHOMA, LEUKAEMIA, SARCOMA, MELANOMA, METASTA, MYELOMA, MALIGNAN, ADENOMA, MENINGIOMA, CRANIOPHARYNGIOMA, GLIOMA, ASTROCYTOMA, MEDULLOBLASTOMA, GERMINOMA, EPENDYMOMA, BLASTOMA; subgroup "Malignancy": the tesamorelin malignancy definition (MALIGNAN, CARCINOMA, CANCER, LYMPHOMA, LEUKAEMIA, SARCOMA, MELANOMA, METASTA, MYELOMA, or NEOPLASM without BENIGN). Subgroup "CNS/pituitary tumour" (tumour PTs containing BRAIN, PITUITARY, CRANIOPHARYNGIOMA, GLIOMA, MENINGIOMA, ASTROCYTOMA, MEDULLOBLASTOMA, GERMINOMA, EPENDYMOMA, CENTRAL NERVOUS SYSTEM). Recurrence/progression PTs flagged.
- Hyperglycaemia/type 2 diabetes: Hyperglycaemia; Blood glucose increased; Diabetes mellitus; Type 2 diabetes mellitus; Diabetes mellitus inadequate control; Glycosylated haemoglobin increased; Glucose tolerance impaired; Impaired fasting glucose; Insulin resistance; Diabetic ketoacidosis; Blood glucose abnormal. (Type 1 diabetes mellitus reported separately.)
- IGF-1 increased: Insulin-like growth factor increased; Blood growth hormone increased. (IGF-1 decreased/abnormal reported descriptively; these may reflect efficacy or dosing.)
- Slipped capital femoral epiphysis/osteonecrosis: Epiphysiolysis; Slipped capital femoral epiphysis; Osteonecrosis; Osteonecrosis of femoral head; Perthes disease; Avascular necrosis (as coded). Gait disturbance and Limp are reported descriptively.
- Scoliosis: Scoliosis; Kyphoscoliosis; Kyphosis; Spinal deformity.
- Intracranial hypertension: Idiopathic intracranial hypertension; Intracranial pressure increased; Benign intracranial hypertension; Papilloedema.
- Fluid retention/musculoskeletal: Oedema peripheral; Oedema; Peripheral swelling; Fluid retention; Swelling; Generalised oedema; Arthralgia; Pain in extremity; Myalgia; Joint swelling; Musculoskeletal stiffness; Musculoskeletal pain; Paraesthesia; Hypoaesthesia; Carpal tunnel syndrome.
- Hypothyroidism: Hypothyroidism; Central hypothyroidism; Secondary hypothyroidism; Thyroxine free decreased; Thyroxine decreased; Blood thyroid stimulating hormone decreased; Tri-iodothyronine free decreased.
- Adrenal insufficiency: Adrenal insufficiency; Secondary adrenocortical insufficiency; Adrenocortical insufficiency acute; Blood cortisol decreased; Glucocorticoid deficiency.
- Pancreatitis: Pancreatitis; Pancreatitis acute; Pancreatitis chronic.
- Headache (alone) and Pregnancy exposure (Exposure during pregnancy; Maternal exposure during pregnancy; Pregnancy; Abortion spontaneous) descriptively.
Non-clinical PTs (product/device issues, product storage/handling, dose omission and other dosing/medication-error terms, off-label use, drug ineffective, therapy interruption, insurance and circumstance terms; pattern list in code, reused from the tesamorelin study) are excluded from clinical signal ranking and analysed as a separate objective (2d), in five prespecified subcategories: (1) dose omission/missed or delayed dose; (2) device issues (DEVICE, INJECTOR, CARTRIDGE, NEEDLE, SYRINGE); (3) product storage, temperature, expiry and preparation/reconstitution; (4) incorrect dose, wrong technique, wrong schedule, wrong drug/product, accidental exposure; (5) product quality, counterfeit, supply and access. Group-level disproportionality is computed for the whole non-clinical set and each subcategory versus all drugs and versus comparators. "Long-term safety/mPEG exposure" has no PT definition; it is addressed descriptively (reports with treatment duration >= 2 years from THER and their PTs) and stated as not assessable in FAERS. If, after first output, further uninformative terms appear in the signal list, any reclassification will be reported as a deviation. PTs that may reflect the treated condition (growth retardation, short stature, body height below normal, growth hormone deficiency, IGF-1 decreased, therapeutic response decreased) are flagged.

## 8. Disproportionality methods and thresholds
2x2 per PT: a = lonapegsomatropin reports with the PT; b = without; c = other reports with the PT; d = other reports without (same 2021 Q3-2026 Q3 window).
- ROR (95% CI, log method); PRR with Yates chi-square (Evans: PRR >= 2, chi-square >= 4, a >= 3); BCPNN IC = log2[(a+0.5)/(E+0.5)] with IC025 by Norén's closed-form approximation; MGPS (DuMouchel 1999) two-component gamma-mixture prior fitted to all drug-PT pairs in the window (drug = PROD_AI, else DRUGNAME; PS rows), EBGM and EB05. If the zero-truncated likelihood is degenerate, the non-truncated fit is used and reported.
- Primary signal: a >= 3, ROR025 > 1 and IC025 > 0. High-specificity: additionally EB05 >= 2.
- Active comparator (lonapegsomatropin vs daily somatropin; vs somatrogon): ROR with Haldane 0.5 correction when a cell is 0; a >= 3 rule; lower reporting when upper CI < 1. Group-level (any PT in group, report level) for all prespecified groups, versus all drugs and versus comparators.
- Pediatric vs adult: PT and group analyses within age strata (<18; >=18), each against the same-stratum background; strata compared by the ratio of RORs with 95% CI (log-scale z test).
- No multiplicity adjustment; signals are hypothesis-generating.

## 9. Time to onset
TTO = EVENT_DT minus START_DT (THER, matching the lonapegsomatropin DRUG_SEQ), day-complete dates only; negative and > 5-year values excluded. Median (IQR), share within 30 days, Weibull shape (95% CI). Overall and for injection-site, hypersensitivity, glycaemic, SCFE/osteonecrosis, scoliosis and intracranial hypertension groups (when n >= 5).

## 10. Descriptive analyses
Sex, age (<6, 6-11, 12-17, 18-64, >=65, unknown), reporter occupation, country, report type, manufacturer vs direct reports, FDA receipt quarter, indication (INDI), dose and route (DRUG), serious outcomes (OUTC). Reports per quarter for lonapegsomatropin and comparators; for the reporting surge, the 2026 Q3 count is compared with the mean of 2025 Q3-2026 Q2 and the composition of 2026 Q3 (age band, reporter, country, report type, non-clinical share, top PTs) is compared with earlier quarters. Adult reports before vs after the adult approval (label revision 7/2025).

## 11. Labeled vs unlabeled
Labeled terms (Skytrofa US label, BLA 761177, openFDA version 14, effective 21 October 2026, sections 4-8): increased mortality in acute critical illness; severe hypersensitivity (anaphylaxis, angioedema; hypersensitivity PTs); neoplasms (all neoplasm group PTs, including second neoplasm, meningioma, nevus changes); glucose intolerance/diabetes mellitus (hyperglycaemia group, type 1 not labeled unless "diabetes mellitus"); diabetic retinopathy; intracranial hypertension with papilloedema, visual changes, headache, nausea, vomiting; fluid retention: oedema, arthralgia, myalgia, carpal tunnel syndrome, paraesthesia; hypoadrenalism; hypothyroidism (central); slipped capital femoral epiphysis and osteonecrosis; scoliosis progression; pancreatitis/abdominal pain; lipoatrophy; injection-site reactions; sudden death in Prader-Willi syndrome; pediatric table: viral infection, pyrexia, cough, nausea and vomiting, haemorrhage (epistaxis, contusion, petechiae, eye haemorrhage), diarrhoea, abdominal pain, arthralgia and arthritis; adult table: oedema peripheral, peripheral swelling, central hypothyroidism, thyroxine (free) decreased, TSH decreased, tri-iodothyronine free decreased; laboratory: alkaline phosphatase, phosphate and parathyroid hormone increased; IGF-1 increased (dosing section, monitoring). Exact/synonym mapping documented in code; otherwise unlabeled.

## 13. Part B: systematic review
Sources: MEDLINE (PubMed), Embase (UBC Library), Europe PMC (includes preprints and indexed conference abstracts), ClinicalTrials.gov results, Endocrine Society (J Endocr Soc supplements), ESPE and ESE (Endocrine Abstracts) and PES abstracts, Google Scholar (first 200 results by relevance), FDA (BLA 761177 reviews and label history), EMA (EPAR, PSUSA scientific conclusions, RMP summary) and TGA (AusPAR), plus reference lists. Inception to search date; no language limit if an English abstract or reliable translation exists.
PubMed: (lonapegsomatropin[tiab] OR Skytrofa[tiab] OR "TransCon GH"[tiab] OR "TransCon hGH"[tiab] OR "TransCon growth hormone"[tiab] OR "ACP-011"[tiab] OR "ACP-001"[tiab] OR lonapegsomatropin[nm]). Equivalent free-text strings elsewhere.
Eligibility: Stream 1, case reports, case series, letters or abstracts describing >= 1 human with an adverse event temporally associated with lonapegsomatropin. Stream 2, interventional trials (phase 2-3 and extensions: e.g., heiGHt, fliGHt, enliGHten, foresiGHt, the adult phase 2 and 3 trials and their extension, the China phase 3 trial, the Turner syndrome trial) and observational registries/cohorts reporting safety outcomes of lonapegsomatropin in humans. Excluded: reviews without new data, pharmacokinetic-only studies without safety outcomes, animal/in vitro studies, economic studies.
Screening/extraction: one reviewer with AI-assisted checking (no unpublished or patient-level data sent to third-party AI tools); a second author verifies inclusion decisions and extracted fields (verification, not independent duplicate review). Full texts are obtained (open access or UBC Library) for all included records; abstracts alone are used only if no full text exists, and this is stated. Stream 1 extracted: country, age, sex, indication, dose, duration, event, latency, investigations, dechallenge/rechallenge, treatment, outcome; JBI appraisal; Naranjo causality. Stream 2 extracted: design, population, N exposed, duration, TEAEs, serious AEs, discontinuations for AEs, deaths, and the prespecified event groups (injection-site reactions, lipoatrophy, hypersensitivity, IGF-1 SDS > +2, glucose/HbA1c, hypothyroidism, adrenal insufficiency, neoplasms, SCFE, scoliosis, intracranial hypertension, anti-drug antibodies); harms-reporting quality summarised with McHarm items.
Synthesis: narrative and tabular by event group; trial counts summarised per group across trials without formal meta-analysis (exposure differs).

## 14. Integration
For each prespecified group: concordant (FAERS signal and >= 1 published case or trial excess), FAERS-only, literature/trial-only, neither.

## 15. Limitations acknowledged in advance
Under-reporting, stimulated and manufacturer-programme reporting (patient support programmes generate dose-omission and device reports), no denominator, channelling and confounding by indication (organic GHD, prior CNS tumour, cranial irradiation), switches between GH products, residual duplicates, missing age and dates, small counts, short post-approval window, notoriety after regulatory communications; disproportionality is not risk. Long-term mPEG effects cannot be evaluated in FAERS.

## 16. Deviations
Any deviation will be dated and reported in the manuscript.

## 17. Ethics and data
Public de-identified FAERS data; no ethics approval needed. Code and aggregate outputs will be shared on request and in a public repository on acceptance; no case-level or unpublished patient data will be sent to third-party AI tools.
